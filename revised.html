IエグゼクティブサマリーExecutive SummaryBuod ng Proyekto

本改訂版は、Aklan州における資本 ₱2,000,000 の中規模養豚事業計画です。元の構想（フィリピンの豚肉需要は安定＝堅実）は妥当ですが、本版は初稿の3つの弱点を修正しました：① 楽観的すぎる投資回収、② アフリカ豚熱（ASF）対策の欠如、③ キャッシュフロー・リスク分析の欠落。

事業はまず肥育専業（子豚を購入して育てる）として開始し、早期の資金回収と低リスクを実現。その後、利益を再投資して第2フェーズで繁殖母豚を追加します。現実的な投資回収は1〜2年ではなく3〜4年。生死は最優先事項であるASFバイオセキュリティにかかっています。

This revised proposal establishes a medium-scale piggery in Aklan with capital of ₱2,000,000. The original concept is sound — pork demand is stable — but this edition corrects three weaknesses in the first draft: an over-optimistic payback claim, the absence of an ASF defense plan, and the lack of cash-flow and risk modeling.

It starts as a grower-finisher operation (buying weaners) for faster cash flow and lower risk, then reinvests profits to add breeding sows in Phase 2. Realistic payback is 3–4 years, not 1–2. Survival depends on ASF biosecurity, the top priority.

Ang binagong panukalang ito ay nagtatatag ng katamtamang laki na piggery sa Aklan na may puhunang ₱2,000,000. Maganda ang orihinal na konsepto — matatag ang demand sa baboy — ngunit inayos ng bersyong ito ang tatlong kahinaan ng unang draft: sobrang optimistikong pagbawi ng puhunan, kawalan ng plano laban sa ASF, at kawalan ng cash-flow at pagsusuri ng panganib.

Magsisimula ito bilang grower-finisher (pagbili ng weaner) para sa mas mabilis na cash flow at mas mababang panganib, pagkatapos ay muling iiinvest ang kita upang magdagdag ng breeding sows sa Phase 2. Ang makatotohanang pagbawi ng puhunan ay 3–4 taon, hindi 1–2. Nakasalalay ang kaligtasan sa biosecurity laban sa ASF, ang pinakaunang prayoridad.

IV市場分析Market AnalysisPagsusuri ng Merkado

豚肉は主要タンパク源で需要は安定。バイヤー：Aklanの生鮮市場、精肉販売業者、レストラン、施設バイヤー。衛生管理・安定品質・直接販売で差別化。裏庭養豚や大規模農場との競争は、信頼性とバイオセキュリティ主導の群健康で緩和。

Pork is a staple protein with stable demand. Buyers: local wet markets, meat vendors, restaurants, and institutional buyers in Aklan. Differentiation through sanitation, consistent quality, and direct selling. Competition from backyard and large farms is mitigated by reliability and biosecurity-driven herd health.

Ang baboy ay pangunahing protina na may matatag na demand. Mga buyer: wet market, meat vendor, restawran, at institutional buyer sa Aklan. Pagkakaiba sa pamamagitan ng sanitasyon, tuloy-tuloy na kalidad, at direktang pagbebenta. Ang kumpetisyon mula sa backyard at malalaking farm ay napapagaan ng pagiging maaasahan at malusog na kawan dahil sa biosecurity.

V段階的生産戦略Phased Production StrategyHakbang-hakbang na Produksyon

なぜ段階的か：初日から一貫経営を始めると初収入が約9〜11ヶ月後まで遅れ、経験のない段階で母豚に資本が固定される。肥育専業なら4〜5ヶ月で資金が戻り、先にスキルを構築できる。

Why phased: Full farrow-to-finish from day one delays first revenue to ~9–11 months and ties up capital in sows before the operator has experience. Grower-finisher returns cash in 4–5 months and builds skill first.

Bakit hakbang-hakbang: Ang buong farrow-to-finish mula sa unang araw ay naaantala ang unang kita hanggang ~9–11 buwan at nagkakandado ng puhunan sa sows bago pa magkaranas ang operator. Ang grower-finisher ay nagbabalik ng pera sa 4–5 buwan at nagtatayo muna ng kasanayan.

VIバイオセキュリティ＆ASF対策 ― 最優先Biosecurity & ASF Prevention — Top PriorityBiosecurity at ASF — Pinakamahalaga

なぜ譲れないのか：アフリカ豚熱は2019年以降、フィリピンの小規模養豚場を繰り返し全滅させてきた。40〜60頭規模では、一度の発生＝群と投資の全損。治療法は存在せず、予防のみが唯一の手段。 Why non-negotiable: ASF has repeatedly wiped out small Philippine piggeries since 2019. For 40–60 heads, one outbreak means total loss of herd and investment. There is no treatment — only prevention. Bakit hindi mapagkakasunduan: Paulit-ulit nang nawasak ng ASF ang maliliit na piggery sa Pilipinas mula 2019. Sa 40–60 na baboy, isang outbreak = kabuuang pagkawala ng kawan at puhunan. Walang lunas — pag-iwas lamang ang tanging paraan.

🆕 なぜこの厳格な統治構造か：「資金を一括で渡したが、報告も成果も来ない」失敗は実在する。トランチ・専用口座・月次写真報告は、運営者を縛るためでなく双方の信頼を仕組みで担保し、資金が物（豚舎・豚・飼料）に変わっていることを可視化するもの。誠実な運営者にはむしろ追加出資を引き出しやすくする装置になる。 🆕 Why this governance: "Money handed over in one lump, then no reports and no results" is a real failure mode. Tranches, a dedicated account, and monthly photo reports guarantee mutual trust by design and make visible that funds are turning into real assets. For an honest operator this makes follow-on funding easier. 🆕 Bakit ganitong pamamahala: Totoong nangyayari ang "naibigay ang pera nang buo, tapos walang ulat at resulta." Ang tranches, nakalaang account, at buwanang litrato ay ginagarantiya ang tiwala at ipinapakita na nagiging tunay na ari-arian ang pera. Para sa tapat na operator, mas madali nitong makukuha ang dagdag na pondo.
