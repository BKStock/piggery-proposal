🩹直すべき点 ①：収益が「うますぎる話」になっているFix #1: Your Revenue Is a "Too-Good" StoryAyusin #1: Sobrang Ganda ng Iyong Kita

最重要CRITICALKRITIKAL

「40頭×100kg×₱180＝₱720,000」と書きましたね。これが危険です。You wrote "40 × 100kg × ₱180 = ₱720,000." This is dangerous.Isinulat mo ang "40 × 100kg × ₱180 = ₱720,000." Mapanganib ito.

なぜ？ この計算は「豚が1頭も死なず、全頭が満額で売れる」という、現実には起きない理想を前提にしています。投資家は養豚の現実を知っています。離乳前に10〜15%は死ぬ、と。Why? It assumes zero pigs die and all sell at full price — which never happens. Investors know piggery reality: 10–15% die before weaning.Bakit? Ipinapalagay nito na walang baboy na mamamatay at lahat ay mabebenta sa buong presyo — na hindi nangyayari. Alam ng investor ang katotohanan: 10–15% ang namamatay bago mag-wean.

こうしてください：Do this:Gawin ito: 死亡率5%を前提に「38頭販売」で計算し直す。総収入₱684,000、コスト₱560,000を引いて純利益は1サイクル約₱124,000。この「正直な数字」を書けば、投資家は逆にあなたを信用します。 Recalculate with 5% mortality → "38 sold." Gross ₱684,000 minus ₱560,000 cost = net ≈ ₱124,000 per cycle. Writing this "honest number" actually makes investors trust you. Muling kalkulahin gamit ang 5% patay → "38 nabenta." Gross ₱684,000 bawas ₱560,000 gastos = net ≈ ₱124,000 kada cycle. Ang pagsulat ng "tapat na numerong" ito ay nagpapatiwala sa investor sa iyo.

🩹直すべき点 ②：最初の半年を生き延びるお金がないFix #2: No Money to Survive the First 6 MonthsAyusin #2: Walang Pera Para Mabuhay ang Unang 6 na Buwan

最重要CRITICALKRITIKAL

子豚を買ってから売れるまで4〜5ヶ月。その間、収入はゼロです。It takes 4–5 months from buying weaners to selling. During that time, income is zero.4–5 buwan mula pagbili ng weaner hanggang mabenta. Sa panahong iyon, zero ang kita.

なぜ？ でも飼料代も人件費も毎月出ていきます。あなたの予算₱2,000,000には「最初の収入が入るまでの繋ぎ資金（運転資金）」の項目がありません。これだと初年度に必ず資金ショートします。これは養豚で一番多い失敗パターンです。Why? But feed and labor go out every month. Your ₱2M budget has no line for "bridge money until first income" (working capital). You WILL run out of cash in year one. This is the #1 failure mode in piggery.Bakit? Ngunit lumalabas ang feed at sahod kada buwan. Ang iyong ₱2M na badyet ay walang linya para sa "pam-tawid na pera hanggang unang kita" (working capital). Tiyak na mauubusan ka ng pera sa unang taon. Ito ang #1 na sanhi ng pagkabigo sa piggery.

こうしてください：Do this:Gawin ito: 予算の中に 運転資金₱230,000 ＋ 予備費₱100,000 = 計₱330,000 を必ず確保してください。建物や設備を少し小さくしてでも、この現金クッションを優先する。1回の悪いサイクルで事業が死ぬのを防ぎます。 Reserve ₱230,000 working capital + ₱100,000 contingency = ₱330,000 in your budget. Even if you build slightly smaller, prioritize this cash cushion. It stops one bad cycle from killing the business. Maglaan ng ₱230,000 working capital + ₱100,000 contingency = ₱330,000 sa iyong badyet. Kahit bahagyang liitan ang gusali, unahin ang cash cushion na ito. Pinipigilan nito na patayin ng isang masamang cycle ang negosyo.

🩹直すべき点 ③：ASF（アフリカ豚熱）対策がほぼ書かれていないFix #3: Almost No ASF (African Swine Fever) PlanAyusin #3: Halos Walang Plano Laban sa ASF

最重要CRITICALKRITIKAL

ASFをリスク欄に1行書いただけ。これは一番怖い見落としです。You mentioned ASF in just one line. This is the scariest oversight.Binanggit mo ang ASF sa isang linya lang. Ito ang pinakanakakatakot na napalampas.

なぜ？ ASFは2019年以降、フィリピンの小規模養豚場を何度も全滅させてきました。40〜60頭規模なら、一度の感染で群も投資も全損。治療法はありません。予防だけが命綱です。「厳格なバイオセキュリティ」という抽象的な言葉では投資家は納得しません。Why? ASF has repeatedly wiped out small Philippine piggeries since 2019. At 40–60 heads, one outbreak = total loss of herd and investment. There is no cure — only prevention. The vague phrase "strict biosecurity" will not satisfy an investor.Bakit? Paulit-ulit nang nawasak ng ASF ang maliliit na piggery sa Pilipinas mula 2019. Sa 40–60 ulo, isang outbreak = kabuuang pagkawala. Walang lunas — pag-iwas lamang. Hindi sapat sa investor ang malabong "strict biosecurity."

こうしてください：Do this:Gawin ito: ASFを独立した1つの章にして、具体的な手順を書く：①周囲フェンス＋単一入口 ②ゲートでの足消毒・車両消毒 ③外部の豚肉持ち込み禁止（残飯給餌禁止）④子豚はASF清浄・認証農場からのみ・健康証明書を要求 ⑤新規導入豚は14日間隔離 ⑥バッチ間の完全清掃消毒 ⑦専用の長靴・衣類。さらに「発生時の初動」（隔離→州獣医/BAIに連絡→出入り全停止→記録）も書いておく。 Make ASF its own chapter with concrete steps: ①perimeter fence + single gate ②foot & vehicle disinfection ③no outside pork (no swill feeding) ④source weaners only from ASF-free certified farms with health certificates ⑤14-day quarantine for new pigs ⑥full clean-disinfect between batches ⑦dedicated boots/clothing. Also write an "outbreak response" (isolate → call Provincial Vet/BAI → stop all movement → document). Gawing sariling kabanata ang ASF na may konkretong hakbang: ①bakod + isang gate ②disinfection ng paa at sasakyan ③bawal ang baboy mula labas (bawal swill) ④weaner mula lang sa ASF-free certified farm na may health certificate ⑤14-araw na quarantine sa bagong baboy ⑥linis-disimpekta sa pagitan ng batch ⑦nakalaang bota/damit. Magsulat din ng "tugon sa outbreak" (ihiwalay → tawagan ang Provincial Vet/BAI → itigil ang galaw → idokumento).

⏱️投資回収について：「1〜2年」は書き直してくださいOn Payback: Rewrite the "1–2 Years"Tungkol sa Pagbawi: Baguhin ang "1–2 Taon"

計画書には「1〜2年で投資回収」と書いてありますが、あなた自身の数字と矛盾します。1サイクル純益₱124,000、年2サイクルなら年₱250,000。₱2,000,000を割ると回収は約8年です。2年で回収するには年₱1,000,000の純益が必要で、これは今の規模では物理的に不可能です。Your plan says "payback in 1–2 years," but this contradicts your own numbers. Net ₱124,000/cycle × 2 cycles = ₱250,000/year. Divided into ₱2,000,000 = about 8 years. Two-year payback needs ₱1,000,000/year net — physically impossible at this scale.Sinasabi ng plano mong "pagbawi sa 1–2 taon," ngunit sumasalungat ito sa sarili mong numero. Net ₱124,000/cycle × 2 = ₱250,000/taon. Hatiin sa ₱2,000,000 = humigit-kumulang 8 taon. Ang 2-taong pagbawi ay nangangailangan ng ₱1,000,000/taon — imposible sa laki na ito.

🤝もし他人からお金を集めるならIf You Are Raising Money From SomeoneKung Manghihingi Ka ng Pera

投資家やパートナーから資金を入れてもらう場合、彼らが一番気にするのは「自分のお金がちゃんと豚と豚舎に変わるか」です。次の3つを計画書に入れると、信頼が一気に上がります。If an investor or partner funds you, their #1 worry is "will my money actually turn into pigs and pens?" Adding these three things will sharply increase their trust.Kung may investor o partner na magpopondo, ang #1 alalahanin nila ay "magiging tunay bang baboy at kulungan ang pera ko?" Ang pagdaragdag ng tatlong ito ay magpapataas ng tiwala nila.

  • 分割（トランチ）出資：一括でなく「建設完了→写真確認→次の資金」と段階で渡す形にする
  • 専用の事業口座：個人のお金と混ぜない。事業の出入りが透明になる
  • 月次報告：毎月、財務・頭数・死亡率・銀行残高・写真を投資家に送る
  • 利益配分・撤退条件を先に書面化：所有比率、利益の分け方、そして「死亡率○%超やASF全滅時にどうするか（追加出資か清算か、残資金の扱い）」を最初に決めておく
  • Tranche funding: release in stages — "build done → photos verified → next funds" — not one lump sum
  • Dedicated business account: never mix with personal money; keeps cash flow transparent
  • Monthly reports: send financials, headcount, mortality, bank balance, and photos every month
  • Profit split & exit terms in writing first: ownership %, how profit is shared, and "what happens at >X% mortality or ASF total loss" (more funding or wind-down, and how leftover funds are handled)
  • Hatihати na pondo: ilabas nang paunti-unti — "tapos ang build → na-verify ang litrato → susunod na pondo" — hindi isang buo
  • Nakalaang business account: huwag haluan ng personal na pera; nananatiling transparent ang cash flow
  • Buwanang ulat: ipadala ang pananalapi, bilang, patay, bank balance, at litrato kada buwan
  • Hati sa kita at exit terms muna sa papel: % ng pagmamay-ari, paghahati ng kita, at "ano ang mangyayari sa >X% patay o ASF total loss" (dagdag na pondo o wind-down, at paano ang natirang pera)
